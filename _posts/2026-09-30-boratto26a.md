abstract: Recent EU Commission proceedings against TikTok, Meta, Shein, and Temu have
  framed addictive design as a systemic risk under the Digital Services Act, yet the
  connection to recommender systems remains underexplored. This contribution proposes
  a four-stage analytical framework centered on relevance estimation, session sequencing,
  objective configuration, and interface and notifications, mapping where addictive
  effects emerge in the recsys pipeline and how they can be audited. Drawing on the
  2025 DSA audit and systemic risk assessment reports of six VLOPs, we identify gaps
  in current transparency practices, including no disclosure of optimisation objectives
  and impact-relevant metrics, and derive recommendations for DSA enforcement.
